# Limits

OpenOrange runs your Workers and Durable Objects on Rivet, so Rivet's limits apply. They are
sane defaults, and most are soft limits that you can raise if you need to. If one gets in your
way, [contact Rivet](https://rivet.dev/support/).

**[Read Rivet's limits →](https://rivet.dev/actors/docs/limits/)**
